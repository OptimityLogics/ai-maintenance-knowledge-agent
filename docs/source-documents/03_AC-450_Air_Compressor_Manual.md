# AC-450 Air Compressor: Operation and Maintenance Manual

**Document:** MAN-AC450 | **Revision:** 4 | **Plant:** Utility Room  
**Notice:** Synthetic sample data for training use.

## 1. Machine overview
The AC-450 is a 45 kW oil-injected rotary screw compressor supplying plant compressed air at 7.5 bar. It is direct-driven through a flexible coupling and bolted to a vibration-damped base.

## 2. Normal operating values
- Discharge pressure: 7.5 bar
- Discharge air temperature: 80–95 °C; E58 above 105 °C
- Normal vibration: below 4.5 mm/s
- High-vibration alarm E45: 7.1 mm/s or higher
- Minimum oil pressure: 2.0 bar; E08 below this
- Mounting bolt torque: 85 Nm

## 3. Daily checks
- Drain condensate from the receiver.
- Check compressor oil level.
- Listen for unusual noise or vibration.

## 4. Weekly and monthly maintenance
- Weekly: check the air filter indicator and clean the cooler.
- Monthly: check mounting bolt torque (85 Nm) and inspect the coupling insert for wear or cracks.

## 5. Safety notes
Release all air pressure completely before opening any line. Hearing protection is mandatory in the utility room. Follow SOP-001 Lockout/Tagout before maintenance. Error-code details are in DOC-ERR-001.
