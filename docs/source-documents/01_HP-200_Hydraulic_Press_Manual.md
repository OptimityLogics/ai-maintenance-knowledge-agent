# HP-200 Hydraulic Press: Operation and Maintenance Manual

**Document:** MAN-HP200 | **Revision:** 3 | **Plant:** Line 2  
**Notice:** Synthetic sample data for training use.

## 1. Machine overview
The HP-200 is a 200-tonne hydraulic press used for forming and stamping sheet-metal parts on Line 2. It has a 120-litre hydraulic oil tank, a 37 kW pump motor and an oil-to-air cooler with an electric cooling fan.

## 2. Normal operating values
- Maximum system pressure: 200 bar
- Normal oil temperature: 40–55 °C
- Oil temperature alarm (E45): 65 °C
- Hydraulic oil grade: ISO VG 46
- Oil tank capacity: 120 litres
- Minimum oil level: midpoint of the sight glass

## 3. Daily checks
- Check the oil level in the sight glass before starting.
- Inspect hoses and fittings for oil leaks.
- Confirm the light curtain and safety door interlock work.
- Listen for unusual pump noise.

## 4. Weekly and periodic maintenance
- Weekly: clean oil cooler fins with compressed air and check the cooling fan turns freely.
- Monthly: check the oil filter pressure indicator and take an oil sample.
- Every 2,000 operating hours: replace the return filter and hydraulic oil.

## 5. Safety notes
Never open hydraulic lines while the system is pressurised. Hot oil can cause serious burns. Follow SOP-001 Lockout/Tagout before maintenance. Error-code details are in DOC-ERR-001.
