# CV-310 Belt Conveyor: Operation and Maintenance Manual

**Document:** MAN-CV310 | **Revision:** 2 | **Plant:** Packing to Dispatch  
**Notice:** Synthetic sample data for training use.

## 1. Machine overview
The CV-310 is a 12-metre belt conveyor moving packed cartons from packing to dispatch. It has a 600 mm belt, a 5.5 kW geared motor, 18 idler rollers and a screw take-up tensioner at the tail end.

## 2. Normal operating values
- Belt speed: 0.5 m/s
- Maximum load: 40 kg/m
- Belt tracking tolerance: within 15 mm of centre line
- Belt misalignment error E45: drift greater than 25 mm
- Normal motor current: 9–11 A; E20 above 14 A
- Gearbox oil grade: ISO VG 220

## 3. Daily checks
- Check belt tracking against the green guide marks.
- Clear debris from under and around the belt.
- Test the emergency pull cord along the full length.

## 4. Weekly and monthly maintenance
- Weekly: check each idler roller turns freely by hand, check both tension bolts and lubricate take-up screws.
- Monthly: check gearbox oil level and inspect belt edges for wear.

## 5. Safety notes
Keep hands away from pinch points between belt and rollers. Lock out the conveyor following SOP-001 before clearing a jam or adjusting the belt. Error-code details are in DOC-ERR-001.
