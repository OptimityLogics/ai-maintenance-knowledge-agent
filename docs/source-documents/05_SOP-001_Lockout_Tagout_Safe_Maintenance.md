# SOP-001: Lockout/Tagout and Safe Maintenance

**Standard Operating Procedure:** SOP-001 | **Revision:** 5  
**Notice:** Synthetic sample data for training use.

## 1. Purpose and scope
Protect technicians from unexpected start-up and stored energy during maintenance. Applies to HP-200, CV-310 and AC-450.

## 2. Required protective equipment
- Safety glasses and safety shoes at all times.
- Gloves when handling hot parts or oil.
- Hearing protection near the AC-450 compressor.

## 3. Procedure
1. Inform the supervisor and machine operator.
2. Stop the machine using the normal stop control.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical, hydraulic and compressed air.
4. Apply a personal lock and tag to each isolation point.
5. Release stored energy: bleed hydraulic and air pressure to zero, and remove belt tension.
6. Verify zero energy by trying to start the machine; it must not start.
7. Carry out maintenance.
8. Remove tools, refit all guards and remove your own lock and tag.
9. Inform the operator and restart the machine.

## 4. Machine-specific notes
- **HP-200:** Never open hydraulic lines under pressure. Wait 20 minutes for hot oil to cool before working on the oil system.
- **CV-310:** Release belt tension and lock out before clearing jams or adjusting the belt. Never remove guards while the belt is moving.
- **AC-450:** Fully release receiver and line pressure to 0 bar before opening any line.

## 5. Rules and reporting
Never bypass a safety interlock, light curtain or emergency stop. If a safety device fails, stop work and call the maintenance supervisor immediately. Record every maintenance event in the maintenance incident log.
